Project Description

International IT Solutions partnered with Agrupacion Catolica Universitaria (ACU) to create an innovative digital outreach platform, including both a website and mobile app. This comprehensive platform was designed to increase community engagement by providing a user-friendly experience that reflects the ACU's mission and core values. Our team focused on delivering a cohesive solution that enables their members to access essential resources, stay informed about services and events, and engage in community activities more seamlessly.

We managed the platform's development from design through implementation, integrating an intuitive interface across both web and mobile. We developed several key features, such as an event and volunteer management system with RSVP capabilities and mobile notifications to keep the congregation engaged. Additionally, we built an area where the congregants can access various spiritual resources and publications online, so they can feel more connected in their religion.

With this project, International IT Solutions delivered a meaningful, tech-forward solution that aligns with the values of The ACU. The result is a robust, easy-to-use platform that enhances their online presence, supporting greater connectivity and participation within their community.

Customer Challenges

This project presented several challenges, from ensuring accessibility for all users, including those with disabilities, to safeguarding data privacy, especially for sensitive donations and prayer requests. Creating a seamless user experience across web and mobile platforms required careful design adjustments to maintain consistency and performance. Scalability was also a consideration, as we needed to ensure the platform could handle increased traffic as the community grew. Additionally, engaging community members unfamiliar with digital tools posed usability challenges, necessitating intuitive design and clear instructions. Finally, managing frequent content updates demanded a user-friendly content management system and ongoing support for The ACU staff

  • Ensuring Data Privacy and Security
